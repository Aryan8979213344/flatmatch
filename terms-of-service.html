7. Safety

  • Meet first in a public place, in daytime, and tell a friend or family member where you are going.
  • See the flat in person and read the rental agreement before you pay anything.
  • Never send advance rent, a token amount or a security deposit to someone you have not met and whose ownership or authority you have not checked.
  • Follow local police-verification rules for tenants.
  • In an emergency, call 112 (police and emergency) or 181 (women’s helpline). Report the person to us afterwards.

Safety tips do not make a meeting or a tenancy safe, and we cannot supervise anything that happens offline.
