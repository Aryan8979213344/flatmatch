Privacy Policy

How FlatMatch collects, uses, shares and protects your personal data, under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

The short version

  • We collect only what the app needs: your account, profile, the areas you search, your chats, your flat listings and the photos you choose to add.
  • Other users see only part of your profile at first. Your full name, photo, exact flat address and phone number are revealed only in the steps described in section 5.
  • We never sell your data. We show no ads and use no analytics or advertising trackers.
  • You can edit, hide or delete your data yourself in the app, and you can ask us for access, correction or erasure at any time.
  • FlatMatch is for people aged 18 and over only.

1. Who we are

FlatMatch (“we”, “us”) is run by its founder, Aryan, in India. For the personal data described here, we are the “Data Fiduciary” under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the “DPDP Act”) and the rules made under it. This policy is our notice to you under that law. It also applies alongside the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, so far as they apply. It should be read with our Terms of Service.

You can ask for this notice in Hindi or any other language listed in the Eighth Schedule to the Constitution by writing to us (section 15).

3. Why we use your data

We process your personal data with your consent, which you give when you create your account and tick the consent boxes, and for the “legitimate uses” the DPDP Act allows (for example, to follow the law or a court order, or to respond to a medical emergency or a threat to someone’s safety). We use it to:

  • create and run your account, and let you edit, hide or delete your data;
  • match you with people searching the same area and calculate compatibility scores. This is done automatically from the answers you give. It is a suggestion only and does not make any decision with legal effect about you;
  • show flat listings, deliver requests and real-time chat messages, and reveal the address only as described below;
  • detect and stop brokers, fake listings, scams, harassment and other misuse, and handle reports and complaints;
  • keep the service secure, fix errors and meet our legal duties; and
  • contact you about your account, safety or changes to our terms.

We will ask for fresh consent before using your data for a new purpose that is not covered here.

4. How we collect it

Mainly from you, when you type, choose, upload or send something. Some technical data is collected automatically when you use the site. We do not buy personal data about you from anyone else.

6. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal data. We share it only with:

  • Other users, as set out in section 5.
  • Service providers (“Data Processors”) who handle data for us under contract: Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage and real-time messages); our website host (GitHub Pages or Vercel); the content delivery networks that serve the code libraries the site loads (such as jsDelivr and unpkg), which can see your IP address when your browser loads them; and an email delivery provider, if we send you account emails.
  • Authorities when the law requires, for example a lawful order from a court or government agency, or to prevent or investigate a crime or a threat to safety. We share only what is needed.
  • A buyer or successor if FlatMatch is ever transferred, who must keep your data protected at the same standard, and we will tell you first.

7. Cookies and browser storage

We use your browser’s local storage only to keep you signed in (a sign-in token). We do not use advertising cookies, tracking pixels or analytics tools at present. If we add any, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires.

8. Children

FlatMatch is only for people 18 years or older. We do not knowingly collect personal data of anyone under 18, and we do not track or advertise to children. If we learn that an account belongs to a person under 18, we will delete it and the data in it. If you think a child is using FlatMatch, please tell us (section 15).

9. How long we keep data

  • While your account is active: your profile, listings, requests, messages and shared numbers are kept so that the service works.
  • Area visibility: each area search stays active for 30 days unless you renew it, and expired searches are removed.
  • When you delete your account (Edit profile → Danger zone → Delete account permanently), your profile and photos, flat listings and their photos, requests, messages, shared phone numbers, and blocks and reports linked to your account are deleted, and your sign-in record is removed. This happens immediately and cannot be undone. Copies held in backups or provider logs are overwritten in their normal cycle.
  • Legal needs: if the law requires us to keep something, or an authority has told us to preserve it, we keep only that, for only as long as needed.
  • Inactive accounts: we may delete accounts that have been inactive for a long time, after giving you notice.

10. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you have the right to:

  • Access. Ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we process it, and the names of the other parties we have shared it with.
  • Correct and update. You can change most details yourself under Edit profile. We will fix anything else on request.
  • Erase. Delete your account in the app, or ask us to erase your data. We will do so unless the law requires us to keep it.
  • Withdraw consent. You can withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it, by hiding your profile, removing an area or deleting your account. Withdrawing does not make earlier processing unlawful. Some features will stop working without the data they need.
  • Grievance redressal. Complain to us about how your data is handled (section 15).
  • Nominate. Name another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to act.

How to use these rights: email aryan92290@gmail.com with the subject “Privacy request” from the email address on your account. We may ask you to confirm who you are so that no one else can misuse your account. We aim to reply within 15 days, and in any case within the time the law allows.

11. Your duties

The DPDP Act also asks you to: follow the law; not pretend to be another person; give only true and verifiable information; not hide material facts when you give information for official documents or IDs; not make false or frivolous complaints; and give only authentic details when you ask us to correct or erase data. Breaking these duties can lead to penalties under the Act.

12. Security and data breaches

  • All traffic between your device and our services uses HTTPS (TLS).
  • Passwords are stored in hashed form by our authentication provider.
  • Our database uses row-level access rules, so each user can read only the data they are allowed to see. Profile photos are kept in a private storage area and opened through short-lived links, only for people allowed to see them.
  • We limit who can access the system and keep access to what the work needs.

No online service can be made perfectly secure. If a personal data breach affects you, we will tell you and the Data Protection Board of India in the way and within the time the law requires.

13. Transfers outside India

Our providers may store or process data on servers in India or in other countries. The DPDP Act allows transfer of personal data outside India except to countries the Central Government restricts. We use providers that apply security safeguards, and we will follow any restriction the Government notifies.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the app or the law changes. We will change the date at the top and, if the change matters, tell you in the app or by email. Where the law requires, we will ask for your consent again.
