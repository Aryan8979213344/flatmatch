Privacy Policy for FlatMatch

FlatMatch ("we", "our", or "us") operates the FlatMatch mobile application (Package ID: com.aryan.flatmatch). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you use our platform to find flats, flatmates, and manage shared living expenses in India.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account & Profile Information: Name, age confirmation (18+), gender, occupation/role, city, locality preferences, budget range, move-in timeline, lifestyle preferences, short bio, and profile photo.
  • Flat Listing Details: Property photos, rent, deposit, furnishing status, amenities, locality, and private street address.
  • Communications & Safety Data: In-app chat messages between connected users, voluntary phone number shares, shared flat expense records, and user safety reports or blocked user lists.
  • Authentication & Technical Data: Email address or login credentials managed securely through Supabase Authentication.

2. How We Use & Protect Your Information

  • Consent-Based Privacy: Other users searching your locality only see your first name, short intro, and lifestyle answers. Your exact flat address and phone number remain strictly hidden until you explicitly accept an inquiry or choose to share your phone number inside a private chat.
  • Service Delivery & Safety: We use your data to match compatible flatmates, deliver real-time messages, prevent broker spam, and enforce our community safety standards.
  • No Selling of Data: We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data or phone number to third-party advertisers or brokers.

3. Data Storage & Security

All user data, authentication records, and uploaded images are encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS and stored securely on cloud infrastructure powered by Supabase with strict Row-Level Security (RLS) access controls.

4. Your Rights & In-App Account Deletion

In accordance with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (India), Google Play policies, and Apple App Store guidelines, you have full control over your data:

  • Edit Profile: You may update or modify your profile details and photos at any time inside the app.
  • Permanent Account Deletion: You can permanently delete your account, profile photos, flat listings, and messages directly inside the app by opening Edit Profile → Danger Zone → Delete Account Permanently. Deletion takes effect immediately and cannot be undone.

5. Age Restriction (18+ Only)

FlatMatch is strictly intended for adults aged 18 years and older. We do not knowingly collect personal information from minors.
